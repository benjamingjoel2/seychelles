# Images needed

Named hotels, lodges, restaurants and cafés must be shown with a photo of the real place, taken from
a source we are allowed to use (venue media kit or press page, partner feed, or Creative Commons with credit).

| Venue | Page | What to request | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| All eleven stays in `src/data/stays.js` | `/stays/` | The stays are described by place (for example "A guesthouse in La Passe"), not named, until the real partner properties are chosen. Then ask each for 3–5 media-kit photos (exterior, room, view) with permission for use by travel partners. | Each property's press or trade contact |

No real venue is named on the site today, and the stays cards have no photos, so nothing shows a wrong place.
